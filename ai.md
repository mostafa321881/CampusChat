# AI Usage — CampusChat

## AI Tools

I used ChatGPT during the development of CampusChat.

## How I Used AI

I used AI to:

- Understand the assignment requirements.
- Get help with C# and Blazor code.
- Find and fix errors.
- Help write documentation.

## Difficult Concepts

Two concepts I needed extra help understanding were:

1. **Live updates in Blazor:** AI helped me understand how messages and posts can update automatically without refreshing the browser.
2. **Access control:** AI helped me understand how to check permissions for private threads, direct chats, and auditor access.

## Testing and Learning

I used JetBrains Rider to build and run the application. I also tested the features in different browser sessions.

AI helped me learn more about Blazor, services, and permissions.
